What Pagezy is

Pagezy is a desktop PDF reader and editor made by Faisal Malik at zylio. It runs on your computer and works on the files you open with it. It does not have a server, a website login or any online features.

The only connections Pagezy can make

  • Checking for updates, once a day. It is on by default, and the first screen Pagezy shows lets you switch it off (so does Settings). Pagezy sends one request to GitHub (api.github.com/repos/faisalrasheed442/pagezy-releases/releases) to see whether a newer version exists. If GitHub is busy, it reads the public release feed at github.com/faisalrasheed442/pagezy-releases/releases.atom instead. The request carries no cookies and no identifier; its User-Agent says only Pagezy/<version>. GitHub sees your IP address, as it does for any visit, under GitHub's privacy statement. If you choose to install an update, Pagezy downloads it from the same release and checks it before running it. Nothing is downloaded until you click.
  • Downloads you start yourself. For example an extra OCR language pack. Pagezy tells you what it will download and from where before it starts, and fetches nothing else.

Every connection is written to the network log in Settings, so you can check this for yourself. Normally it shows only the daily update check.

What Pagezy never does

  • Upload, scan or read your documents anywhere but on your computer.
  • Send usage statistics, crash reports, analytics or any identifier.
  • Show ads, or share or sell any information with anyone.
  • Run JavaScript embedded in PDFs, or load remote content from a document.

What stays on your computer

Pagezy keeps its settings, your recent-files list, logs and recovery copies of unsaved work in a folder on your computer (or next to the program, in the portable edition). Only you can see them, and uninstalling can remove them. Logs never leave your computer unless you choose to send one to us yourself.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we use your message only to answer it. We never ask for your documents; if you choose to send a file to show a problem, it is deleted once the problem is fixed.
