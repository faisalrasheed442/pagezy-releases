A PDF app that stays out of your way

Tabs for every document

Open as many PDFs as you like, side by side in tabs, and pick up where you left off.

Search as you type

Ctrl F highlights every match in the document the moment you start typing.

Ctrl K for everything

Type what you want to do and press Enter. Every command shows its shortcut, so you learn as you go.

Easy on the eyes

Light and dark themes, bigger text, and night pages that darken the document without changing the file.

Private by design

Nothing leaves your computer. Settings shows a log of every connection Pagezy has ever made. Normally that is just the daily update check.

Runs from a USB stick

The portable edition keeps its settings in its own folder and writes nothing to the PC.

Also in the box: editing text in place, forms and signatures, true redaction, offline OCR for scans, conversion to Word, Excel and PowerPoint, and comparing two versions. Each release says what it adds.
